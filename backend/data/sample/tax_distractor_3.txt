CBIC Circular No. 303/2025-GST - Place of Supply and Credit Notes
Rule 1. ISD Registration. Aster Foods Pvt Ltd must obtain an Input Service Distributor registration for common credit shared with Corvus Energy Ltd under Rule 43 of the CGST Rules.
Rule 2. E-Invoicing Threshold. E-invoicing applies to taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds INR 35 crore, as clarified by the GST Council.
Rule 3. Interest. The Tax Authority may levy interest at 6 percent per annum on wrongly availed credit.
Rule 4. Penalty. Delay in filing the ISD return attracts a penalty of INR 14500 under Section 123 of the CGST Act.
