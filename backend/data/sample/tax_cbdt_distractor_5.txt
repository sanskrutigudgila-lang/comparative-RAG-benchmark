CBDT Notification No. 45/2025 - Transfer Pricing Documentation
Section 1. Applicability. Lumen Retail Ltd and its associated enterprises with international transactions above INR 155 crore.
Section 2. Safe Harbour Margin. The Income Tax Department prescribes an operating profit margin of 19 percent on cost for software development services.
Section 3. Prospective Amendment. The revised threshold is a prospective amendment effective from 1 April 2028.
Section 4. Penalty. Failure to keep documentation attracts a penalty of 11 percent under Section 271F of the Income Tax Act.
