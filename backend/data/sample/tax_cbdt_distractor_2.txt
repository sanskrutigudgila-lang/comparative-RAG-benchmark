CBDT Notification No. 42/2025 - Transfer Pricing Documentation
Section 1. Applicability. Nimbus Logistics Ltd and its associated enterprises with international transactions above INR 125 crore.
Section 2. Safe Harbour Margin. The Income Tax Department prescribes an operating profit margin of 14 percent on cost for software development services.
Section 3. Prospective Amendment. The revised threshold is a prospective amendment effective from 1 April 2027.
Section 4. Penalty. Failure to keep documentation attracts a penalty of 8 percent under Section 271C of the Income Tax Act.
