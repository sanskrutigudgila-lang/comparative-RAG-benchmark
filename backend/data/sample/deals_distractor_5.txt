Share Purchase Agreement - Acquisition of Lumen Retail Ltd by Borealis Motors Ltd
Clause 1. Parties. Borealis Motors Ltd (the Buyer) acquires 37 percent of Lumen Retail Ltd from its promoters (the Sellers).
Clause 2. Purchase Price. Enterprise value of INR 525 crore subject to valuation under the Companies Act 2013.
Clause 3. Indemnity. Sellers indemnify the Buyer for pre-closing liabilities, capped at INR 70 crore with a basket of INR 16 crore.
Clause 4. Material Adverse Change. A MAC means any event reducing revenue by more than 13 percent; the Buyer may rescind the agreement before closing.
Clause 5. FEMA Compliance. Foreign investment requires Form FC-TRS within 50 days.
