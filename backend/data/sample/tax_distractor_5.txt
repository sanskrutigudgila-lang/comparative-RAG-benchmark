CBIC Circular No. 305/2025-GST - Place of Supply and Credit Notes
Rule 1. ISD Registration. Lumen Retail Ltd must obtain an Input Service Distributor registration for common credit shared with Borealis Motors Ltd under Rule 45 of the CGST Rules.
Rule 2. E-Invoicing Threshold. E-invoicing applies to taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds INR 45 crore, as clarified by the GST Council.
Rule 3. Interest. The Tax Authority may levy interest at 8 percent per annum on wrongly availed credit.
Rule 4. Penalty. Delay in filing the ISD return attracts a penalty of INR 17500 under Section 125 of the CGST Act.
