RBI Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) and PMLA Compliance

Para 1. Customer Due Diligence. Regulated Entities such as Zenith Finance Ltd must complete Customer Due Diligence (CDD) before account opening, as required by Rule 9 of the PML Rules.

Para 2. Enhanced Due Diligence. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) applies to politically exposed persons and to high-risk customers, including beneficial owners holding more than 10 percent of a company.

Para 3. Suspicious Transaction Reports. A Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) must be filed with the Financial Intelligence Unit within 7 days of arriving at a conclusion of suspicion.

Para 4. Penalty. The Reserve Bank may impose a monetary penalty of up to INR 1 crore per contravention under Section 47A of the Banking Regulation Act, and Zenith Finance Ltd's Chief Compliance Officer is accountable.

Para 5. Control Mapping. Internal audit control IA-07 (periodic KYC refresh) maps to Para 1, and control IA-11 (STR escalation matrix) maps to Para 3.
