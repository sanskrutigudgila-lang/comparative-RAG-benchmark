Share Purchase Agreement - Acquisition of Sable Chemicals Pvt Ltd by Helix Textiles Ltd
Clause 1. Parties. Helix Textiles Ltd (the Buyer) acquires 39 percent of Sable Chemicals Pvt Ltd from its promoters (the Sellers).
Clause 2. Purchase Price. Enterprise value of INR 615 crore subject to valuation under the Companies Act 2013.
Clause 3. Indemnity. Sellers indemnify the Buyer for pre-closing liabilities, capped at INR 86 crore with a basket of INR 18 crore.
Clause 4. Material Adverse Change. A MAC means any event reducing revenue by more than 16 percent; the Buyer may rescind the agreement before closing.
Clause 5. FEMA Compliance. Foreign investment requires Form FC-TRS within 52 days.
