CBIC Circular No. 307/2025-GST - Place of Supply and Credit Notes
Rule 1. ISD Registration. Sable Chemicals Pvt Ltd must obtain an Input Service Distributor registration for common credit shared with Helix Textiles Ltd under Rule 47 of the CGST Rules.
Rule 2. E-Invoicing Threshold. E-invoicing applies to taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds INR 55 crore, as clarified by the GST Council.
Rule 3. Interest. The Tax Authority may levy interest at 10 percent per annum on wrongly availed credit.
Rule 4. Penalty. Delay in filing the ISD return attracts a penalty of INR 20500 under Section 127 of the CGST Act.
