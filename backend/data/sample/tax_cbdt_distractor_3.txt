CBDT Notification No. 43/2025 - Transfer Pricing Documentation
Section 1. Applicability. Aster Foods Pvt Ltd and its associated enterprises with international transactions above INR 135 crore.
Section 2. Safe Harbour Margin. The Income Tax Department prescribes an operating profit margin of 16 percent on cost for software development services.
Section 3. Prospective Amendment. The revised threshold is a prospective amendment effective from 1 April 2028.
Section 4. Penalty. Failure to keep documentation attracts a penalty of 9 percent under Section 271D of the Income Tax Act.
