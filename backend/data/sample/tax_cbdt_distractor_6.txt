CBDT Notification No. 46/2025 - Transfer Pricing Documentation
Section 1. Applicability. Borealis Motors Ltd and its associated enterprises with international transactions above INR 165 crore.
Section 2. Safe Harbour Margin. The Income Tax Department prescribes an operating profit margin of 20 percent on cost for software development services.
Section 3. Prospective Amendment. The revised threshold is a prospective amendment effective from 1 April 2027.
Section 4. Penalty. Failure to keep documentation attracts a penalty of 13 percent under Section 271G of the Income Tax Act.
