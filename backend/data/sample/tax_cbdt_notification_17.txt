CBDT Notification No. 17/2025 - Transfer Pricing Safe Harbour

Section 1. Applicability. This notification applies to resident assessees, including Zenith Pharma Ltd and its associated enterprises, entering into international transactions with an aggregate value up to INR 200 crore.

Section 2. Safe Harbour Margin. The Income Tax Department prescribes an operating profit margin of 18 percent on cost for contract research services provided by Indian subsidiaries to foreign principals.

Section 3. Retrospective Clarification. The explanation to Section 2 is a retrospective clarification effective from 1 April 2023 and does not create a new liability.

Section 4. Prospective Amendment. The reduction of the threshold from INR 300 crore to INR 200 crore is a prospective amendment effective from 1 April 2026.

Section 5. Penalty. Failure to maintain contemporaneous documentation attracts a penalty of 2 percent of the value of each international transaction under Section 271G of the Income Tax Act.
