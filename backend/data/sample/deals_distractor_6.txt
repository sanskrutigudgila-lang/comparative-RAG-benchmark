Share Purchase Agreement - Acquisition of Borealis Motors Ltd by Sable Chemicals Pvt Ltd
Clause 1. Parties. Sable Chemicals Pvt Ltd (the Buyer) acquires 38 percent of Borealis Motors Ltd from its promoters (the Sellers).
Clause 2. Purchase Price. Enterprise value of INR 570 crore subject to valuation under the Companies Act 2013.
Clause 3. Indemnity. Sellers indemnify the Buyer for pre-closing liabilities, capped at INR 78 crore with a basket of INR 17 crore.
Clause 4. Material Adverse Change. A MAC means any event reducing revenue by more than 14 percent; the Buyer may rescind the agreement before closing.
Clause 5. FEMA Compliance. Foreign investment requires Form FC-TRS within 51 days.
