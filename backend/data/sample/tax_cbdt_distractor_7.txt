CBDT Notification No. 47/2025 - Transfer Pricing Documentation
Section 1. Applicability. Sable Chemicals Pvt Ltd and its associated enterprises with international transactions above INR 175 crore.
Section 2. Safe Harbour Margin. The Income Tax Department prescribes an operating profit margin of 21 percent on cost for software development services.
Section 3. Prospective Amendment. The revised threshold is a prospective amendment effective from 1 April 2028.
Section 4. Penalty. Failure to keep documentation attracts a penalty of 14 percent under Section 271H of the Income Tax Act.
