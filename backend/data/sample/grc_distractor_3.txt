RBI Circular - KYC Periodic Updation and Reporting for Aster Foods Pvt Ltd
Para 1. Customer Due Diligence. Regulated Entities must complete CDD before onboarding customers, including checks of beneficial owners holding more than 26 percent.
Para 2. Reporting. A Suspicious Transaction Report must be furnished to the Financial Intelligence Unit within 6 working days of detection.
Para 3. Penalty. The Reserve Bank may impose a penalty of up to INR 24 crore for contravention, and the Principal Officer of Aster Foods Pvt Ltd is responsible.
Para 4. Control Mapping. Internal audit control IA-04 maps to Para 1 and control IA-23 maps to Para 2.
