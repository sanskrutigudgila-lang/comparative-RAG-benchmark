RBI Circular - KYC Periodic Updation and Reporting for Sable Chemicals Pvt Ltd
Para 1. Customer Due Diligence. Regulated Entities must complete CDD before onboarding customers, including checks of beneficial owners holding more than 30 percent.
Para 2. Reporting. A Suspicious Transaction Report must be furnished to the Financial Intelligence Unit within 10 working days of detection.
Para 3. Penalty. The Reserve Bank may impose a penalty of up to INR 28 crore for contravention, and the Principal Officer of Sable Chemicals Pvt Ltd is responsible.
Para 4. Control Mapping. Internal audit control IA-02 maps to Para 1 and control IA-27 maps to Para 2.
