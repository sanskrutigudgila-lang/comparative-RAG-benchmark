Share Purchase Agreement - Acquisition of Helix Textiles Ltd by Vega Steel Pvt Ltd
Clause 1. Parties. Vega Steel Pvt Ltd (the Buyer) acquires 31 percent of Helix Textiles Ltd from its promoters (the Sellers).
Clause 2. Purchase Price. Enterprise value of INR 300 crore subject to valuation under the Companies Act 2013.
Clause 3. Indemnity. Sellers indemnify the Buyer for pre-closing liabilities, capped at INR 30 crore with a basket of INR 11 crore.
Clause 4. Material Adverse Change. A MAC means any event reducing revenue by more than 7 percent; the Buyer may rescind the agreement before closing.
Clause 5. FEMA Compliance. Foreign investment requires Form FC-TRS within 45 days.
