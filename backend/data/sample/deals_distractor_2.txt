Share Purchase Agreement - Acquisition of Nimbus Logistics Ltd by Aster Foods Pvt Ltd
Clause 1. Parties. Aster Foods Pvt Ltd (the Buyer) acquires 34 percent of Nimbus Logistics Ltd from its promoters (the Sellers).
Clause 2. Purchase Price. Enterprise value of INR 390 crore subject to valuation under the Companies Act 2013.
Clause 3. Indemnity. Sellers indemnify the Buyer for pre-closing liabilities, capped at INR 46 crore with a basket of INR 13 crore.
Clause 4. Material Adverse Change. A MAC means any event reducing revenue by more than 9 percent; the Buyer may rescind the agreement before closing.
Clause 5. FEMA Compliance. Foreign investment requires Form FC-TRS within 47 days.
