Share Purchase Agreement - Acquisition of Corvus Energy Ltd by Lumen Retail Ltd
Clause 1. Parties. Lumen Retail Ltd (the Buyer) acquires 36 percent of Corvus Energy Ltd from its promoters (the Sellers).
Clause 2. Purchase Price. Enterprise value of INR 480 crore subject to valuation under the Companies Act 2013.
Clause 3. Indemnity. Sellers indemnify the Buyer for pre-closing liabilities, capped at INR 62 crore with a basket of INR 15 crore.
Clause 4. Material Adverse Change. A MAC means any event reducing revenue by more than 11 percent; the Buyer may rescind the agreement before closing.
Clause 5. FEMA Compliance. Foreign investment requires Form FC-TRS within 49 days.
