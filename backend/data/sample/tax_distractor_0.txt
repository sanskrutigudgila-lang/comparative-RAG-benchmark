CBIC Circular No. 300/2025-GST - Place of Supply and Credit Notes
Rule 1. ISD Registration. Helix Textiles Ltd must obtain an Input Service Distributor registration for common credit shared with Vega Steel Pvt Ltd under Rule 40 of the CGST Rules.
Rule 2. E-Invoicing Threshold. E-invoicing applies to taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds INR 20 crore, as clarified by the GST Council.
Rule 3. Interest. The Tax Authority may levy interest at 3 percent per annum on wrongly availed credit.
Rule 4. Penalty. Delay in filing the ISD return attracts a penalty of INR 10000 under Section 120 of the CGST Act.
