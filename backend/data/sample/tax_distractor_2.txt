CBIC Circular No. 302/2025-GST - Place of Supply and Credit Notes
Rule 1. ISD Registration. Nimbus Logistics Ltd must obtain an Input Service Distributor registration for common credit shared with Aster Foods Pvt Ltd under Rule 42 of the CGST Rules.
Rule 2. E-Invoicing Threshold. E-invoicing applies to taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds INR 30 crore, as clarified by the GST Council.
Rule 3. Interest. The Tax Authority may levy interest at 5 percent per annum on wrongly availed credit.
Rule 4. Penalty. Delay in filing the ISD return attracts a penalty of INR 13000 under Section 122 of the CGST Act.
