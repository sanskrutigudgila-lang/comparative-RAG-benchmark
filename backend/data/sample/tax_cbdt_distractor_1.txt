CBDT Notification No. 41/2025 - Transfer Pricing Documentation
Section 1. Applicability. Vega Steel Pvt Ltd and its associated enterprises with international transactions above INR 115 crore.
Section 2. Safe Harbour Margin. The Income Tax Department prescribes an operating profit margin of 13 percent on cost for software development services.
Section 3. Prospective Amendment. The revised threshold is a prospective amendment effective from 1 April 2028.
Section 4. Penalty. Failure to keep documentation attracts a penalty of 7 percent under Section 271B of the Income Tax Act.
