Share Purchase Agreement - Acquisition of Aster Foods Pvt Ltd by Corvus Energy Ltd
Clause 1. Parties. Corvus Energy Ltd (the Buyer) acquires 35 percent of Aster Foods Pvt Ltd from its promoters (the Sellers).
Clause 2. Purchase Price. Enterprise value of INR 435 crore subject to valuation under the Companies Act 2013.
Clause 3. Indemnity. Sellers indemnify the Buyer for pre-closing liabilities, capped at INR 54 crore with a basket of INR 14 crore.
Clause 4. Material Adverse Change. A MAC means any event reducing revenue by more than 10 percent; the Buyer may rescind the agreement before closing.
Clause 5. FEMA Compliance. Foreign investment requires Form FC-TRS within 48 days.
