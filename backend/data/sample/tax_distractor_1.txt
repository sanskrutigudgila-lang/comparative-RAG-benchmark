CBIC Circular No. 301/2025-GST - Place of Supply and Credit Notes
Rule 1. ISD Registration. Vega Steel Pvt Ltd must obtain an Input Service Distributor registration for common credit shared with Nimbus Logistics Ltd under Rule 41 of the CGST Rules.
Rule 2. E-Invoicing Threshold. E-invoicing applies to taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds INR 25 crore, as clarified by the GST Council.
Rule 3. Interest. The Tax Authority may levy interest at 4 percent per annum on wrongly availed credit.
Rule 4. Penalty. Delay in filing the ISD return attracts a penalty of INR 11500 under Section 121 of the CGST Act.
