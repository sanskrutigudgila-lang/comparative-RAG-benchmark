Share Purchase Agreement - Acquisition of Vega Steel Pvt Ltd by Nimbus Logistics Ltd
Clause 1. Parties. Nimbus Logistics Ltd (the Buyer) acquires 33 percent of Vega Steel Pvt Ltd from its promoters (the Sellers).
Clause 2. Purchase Price. Enterprise value of INR 345 crore subject to valuation under the Companies Act 2013.
Clause 3. Indemnity. Sellers indemnify the Buyer for pre-closing liabilities, capped at INR 38 crore with a basket of INR 12 crore.
Clause 4. Material Adverse Change. A MAC means any event reducing revenue by more than 8 percent; the Buyer may rescind the agreement before closing.
Clause 5. FEMA Compliance. Foreign investment requires Form FC-TRS within 46 days.
