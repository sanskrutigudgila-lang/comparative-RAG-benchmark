CBDT Notification No. 44/2025 - Transfer Pricing Documentation
Section 1. Applicability. Corvus Energy Ltd and its associated enterprises with international transactions above INR 145 crore.
Section 2. Safe Harbour Margin. The Income Tax Department prescribes an operating profit margin of 17 percent on cost for software development services.
Section 3. Prospective Amendment. The revised threshold is a prospective amendment effective from 1 April 2027.
Section 4. Penalty. Failure to keep documentation attracts a penalty of 10 percent under Section 271E of the Income Tax Act.
